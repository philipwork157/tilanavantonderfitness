<p class="article-intro"><span class="drop-cap">H</span><span>i, I'm Tilana van Tonder, and I'm so grateful you've found your way here.</span></p>

This website has been a dream on my heart for many years. It brings together two things I've always loved: helping people and living an active, healthy lifestyle. Whether you're just starting your fitness journey, returning after having children, or simply looking for a healthier and more realistic approach to movement and nutrition, I hope you'll feel at home here.

I've always loved both sport and people. Growing up, athletics and netball were a huge part of my life, while music led me to study education and become a teacher. Teaching has always been my passion because I love seeing people grow in confidence, and I believe coaching fitness is simply another way of doing exactly that.

My own journey, however, hasn't been perfect.

During university, I struggled with an unhealthy relationship with food. It began with restricting what I ate, then developed into binge eating and bulimia. Looking back, I realise I wasn't chasing health—I was chasing acceptance. Through years of learning, reading, making mistakes, and educating myself about nutrition and training, I slowly discovered something that changed my life: **my worth is not measured by a number on the scale, my jean size, or my body fat percentage.**

Yes, I still want to be healthy and strong, but today I train because I love what my body can do—not because I'm trying to earn my worth.

Motherhood has shaped that perspective even more. I trained throughout my first pregnancy with my doctor's approval and, six months after giving birth, I ran my fastest marathon in **4 hours and 4 minutes**. Then, just eight and a half months postpartum, I fell pregnant again. As I write this, I'm 17 weeks pregnant, and once again I'm learning to embrace a body that is changing for a beautiful reason.

This business was born during one of the hardest seasons of our lives. From late 2025, our son was repeatedly admitted to hospital. Between hospital stays, recovery at home, limited family responsibility leave, and the financial pressures that come with it, my husband and I made the privileged decision that I would resign from teaching to care for our son and pursue this dream from home.

Although my name is on this website, I could never have done it without my husband. He believed in this dream before I did, built this entire website, encouraged me through every setback, and continues to support me every step of the way. I am also incredibly grateful for my family and friends, whose love, prayers, and encouragement have carried me through every season.

If there's one thing I hope you know, it's this: **I'm not perfect, and I don't want to pretend to be.** I don't have the "perfect" body, I don't always get everything right, and I'm still learning every day. I'm simply someone who loves movement, values health, and wants to live a life that allows me to be strong enough to enjoy every season God blesses me with—especially with my husband, my children, and, one day, hopefully my grandchildren.

If you've ever struggled with food, confidence, pregnancy, postpartum, or simply knowing where to start, I want you to know that you're not alone. My goal isn't to help you chase perfection. It's to help you build a healthy, sustainable lifestyle that fits real life—one that includes children, work, celebrations, rest, and everything in between.

Thank you for being here at the very beginning of this journey. I truly hope this community becomes a place where you feel encouraged, supported, and reminded that becoming healthier isn't about being perfect—it's about showing up, one step at a time.
