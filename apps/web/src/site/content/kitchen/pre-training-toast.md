> Before training, I personally enjoy something simple like sourdough or an English muffin with about 15 g peanut butter, honey and half a banana. Nothing fancy.


I used to train on an empty stomach and then wonder why I was so tired. Food is not something to earn. It helps you train.

Your ideal pre-training snack might look completely different. That is fine.
